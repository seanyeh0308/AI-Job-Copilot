## Website Requirements Before Domain Review

Paddle expects the submitted domain to be live over HTTPS and to clearly show:

- Product or service description.
- Key features and deliverables included with purchase.
- Pricing details or a pricing page.
- Terms and Conditions.
- Refund Policy.
- Privacy Policy.
- Company name or sole proprietor brand/legal name in Terms and Conditions.
- Any custom or enterprise pricing sheet, if applicable.
